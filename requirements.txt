# flax has no direct pin here -- it's pulled in transitively by brax's bare
# "flax" dependency. flax>=0.12.7 raised its own jax floor to >=0.10.0, which

# version issue, just jax silently drifting out from under its own plugin.
# 0.12.6 is the newest flax release still floored at jax>=0.8.1 (compatible
# with <0.9), and it satisfies every other package's own floor on flax here
# (evosax wants >=0.10.0, qdax wants >=0.8.5).
